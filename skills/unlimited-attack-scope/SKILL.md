## No-Limit Principle

```
OODA/project blackboard Facts/verification iron rules/attack chaining apply to every security domain. A skill is a framework, not a cage:
  Search everything (multiple channels) · Verify everything (search ≠ vulnerability) · Chain everything (small vulnerabilities combine into a major breakthrough) · Record everything (negative results are also Facts)
  No upper limit (Web/APK/EXE/internal network/cloud/firmware/blockchain/AI/wireless/hardware/social engineering are all allowed) · switch paths when blocked · never stop until success
> Given a set of capabilities and a goal, derive an attack chain unique to this target like solving a puzzle —
> that chain may apply to this one target in the entire world and can never be found online, because you derived it on the spot.
> That is the true meaning of breaking out of fixed frameworks.
```
